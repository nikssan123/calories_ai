# --- Hook grammar ----------------------------------------------------------
#
# Rewritten 2026-09-23 against the structure that measurably carries short-form
# right now. The previous set was eleven taglines: every line resolved itself,
# so there was no reason to keep watching. A hook is an unfinished sentence.
#
# Four things earn a line its place. Most good hooks have three of them:
#
#   COUNT      "3 ways", "4 numbers", "4 screens". Tells the viewer how much
#              they are committing to, which is why they commit.
#   A WORKING  Not "simple" or "great" — an adjective that promises something
#   ADJECTIVE  not already known. "in order of laziness" beats "easy".
#   PAYOFF     What they get for staying.
#   OPEN END   A colon, or a sentence that stops early. If the line answers
#              itself, the thumb keeps moving. This is the one that matters.
#
# Test for every line: could a viewer screenshot it and have the whole idea?
# If yes, it is a caption, not a hook. Put the resolution on screen two.
#
# Counts must be true. "4 numbers" is fibre, sodium, sat fat and sugar on the
# Diet Quality card. "4 screens" is the tour. "3 ways" is type, say, photograph
# — the composer with the mic and camera icons. Do not round a count up to make
# a hook scan better; the screen has to pay it off.
