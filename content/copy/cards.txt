# The accuracy lines, and why they are phrased against ourselves too.
#
# The 2026 research (NUTRITION 2026, Hengist & Charles) put photo-based
# trackers 250-345 kcal and ~30g fat light per meal, and that finding is now
# the loudest thing in the category. It is tempting to attack it. We cannot:
# this app estimates from photos as well (`food_entries.source = 'photo'`),
# and there is no measurement showing ours is closer. A superiority claim we
# cannot produce a number for is the one credibility hit §0 says not to take.
#
# So the angle is not "theirs is wrong, ours is right". It is that every
# number in this category is an estimate, ours included, and the thing worth
# comparing is what it costs to fix one. That we can demonstrate on screen:
# item-level rows exist precisely so "there was more rice" updates a row
# rather than making you log the meal again. The researcher's own complaint
# -- "I had to correct it because it misinterpreted what's there" -- is a
# description of our correction flow, from someone who is not selling it.
